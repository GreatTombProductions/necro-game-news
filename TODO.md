# AI Youtube
Updated. Now for the YouTube question:

**The AI-YouTube landscape as I understand it:**

There's a spectrum from "obviously AI slop" to "AI-assisted but clearly curated." The difference is usually:
- **Voice**: Robotic TTS vs. natural-sounding or actual human voice
- **Visuals**: Stock footage slideshows vs. purposeful composition
- **Script**: Generic summarization vs. editorial voice with opinions
- **Pacing**: Monotonous vs. dynamic

The channels that work tend to have a clear editorial identity—AI handles production labor, but there's a recognizable perspective. Morty could be that perspective.

**Tool categories to explore:**

**TTS:**
- ElevenLabs is the current quality leader—can clone voices, sounds natural
- Could potentially create a "Morty voice" that's consistent across episodes
- Alternative: You or Megan do voiceover (higher effort, but distinctive)

**Video compositing:**
- Remotion (React-based video generation) if you want code-level control
- Could pull directly from your existing news feed data
- Template approach: intro → news items with game art → outro

**Script generation:**
- You already have the data (news feed, registry)
- LLM layer to transform "these 8 things happened this week" into "Morty's take on the week in necromancy"
- The editorial voice is the differentiator

**The "not low effort" question:**
The Instagram automation works because static images with consistent branding read as intentional. Video is harder—movement reveals cheapness faster. Options:
- Lean into stylization (motion graphics, not fake gameplay)
- Limited animation that looks deliberate
- Morty as animated character (higher investment, stronger identity)
